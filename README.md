# Cuenta-Wallet-Biweekly


Flujo recomendado para ejecutar ambos Gems
Paso 1 — Cargar la información
________________________________________________
I am preparing the WXX Wallet (or DiDi Cuenta) CX Biweekly Executive Review.

Do NOT generate the report yet.

I will provide information in multiple messages.

Classify every piece of information into:

• KPIs
• Customer Insights
• Operational Updates
• Product Updates
• Technology Updates
• Risks
• Incidents
• Vendor Dependencies
• Business Opportunities
• Action Plans

For every item identify:

• What happened
• Why it happened
• Customer impact
• Operational impact
• Business impact

Do not summarize anything yet.
Simply organize all the information until I ask you to generate the report.

__________________________________________________________

Paso 2 — Executive Analysis
_________________________________________________________


Before generating the report, perform an Executive Analysis.

Identify:

• Top 5 executive messages
• Top 5 customer problems
• Top 5 operational improvements
• Top 3 Product opportunities
• Top 3 Technology opportunities
• Top 3 operational risks
• Top 3 vendor dependencies
• Top 5 recommendations leadership should prioritize

Also identify:

What should NOT be included in the executive report because it is too operational or repetitive.

Do not generate the report yet.

______________________________________________________________________

Paso 3 — Generar el reporte

______________________________________________________________________

Generate the complete CX Biweekly Executive Review following the GEM instructions.

Requirements:

• Executive tone
• Leadership audience
• Explain every KPI using:
    - Root Cause
    - Customer Impact
    - Business Impact
    - Action Plan
• Connect similar problems across KPIs whenever applicable.
• Highlight systemic issues instead of isolated events.
• Finish with Executive Recommendations.

Do not omit any section.
